Endgame

The Fallen Emperor, the Frigate, the Queen and Matriarch, and the Harvester: what each one drops and how to clear it.

Fallen Emperor (Pendola Pass)

Drops the Emperor Modulator (needed for Gunsmith IV), Amplification Modules Mk I–V, ARC Data Modules, Motion Cores and other high-tier parts. Patch 2.3 moved the good loot deeper inside, so containers near the entrance are now low-tier.

  1. KeyCraft an Emperor Gateway Conduit at the Workbench. You need its blueprint plus an ARC Conductive Coupler, an ARC Insulated Coupler and an ARC Plated Coupler.
  2. PartsGet the blueprint and couplers from the Redirection condition. Wait for a Damaged Emperor Relay, grab the beacon it drops, set it down somewhere open, stand back, and loot the central Harvester payload. The blueprint is also a rare drop from ARC Couriers.
  3. LoadoutSnap Hook (close to required; bring a Grappling Hook as backup), a Hullcracker for Hydras, an Anvil or Renegade for the yellow nodes, plenty of healing and shield rechargers, and a Safe Pocket augment.
  4. EntryWest door, then a corridor, a Hydra and an electrified elevator.
  5. Layout APillar room: shoot the yellow nodes to lower a pillar and ride it up. Crusher room: wait for the walls to pull back, run to the open end (avoid the stamina-draining gas), turn left, and climb out after the next crush.
  6. Layout BContainer racks: Snap Hook to the top and ride a lowering container up. Laser tunnel: crouch in the center behind the spinning drum and time your moves.
  7. Hydra roomSnap Hook between cover, kill the active turrets with the Hullcracker, and take the upper door.
  8. LootWhen you drop into the final room, a wave of ARC spawns. Hide until they spread out. Loot the containers, then shoot the yellow wall nodes to pop out payload racks; one or two hold the Modulator. Put it in your Safe Pocket and leave through the big hatch.

Shani's quest Belly of the Beast sends you here. Rewards: a Legendary Snap Hook, the Observatory Key, consumables and 10,000 XP.

The Frigate

Event rules

  • A map condition on Dam, Buried City, Spaceport, Blue Gate, Riven Tides and Pendola Pass. Not Stella Montis.
  • No Free Loadout. No Raider Hatches. Locked rooms are off and ground loot is reduced.
  • Still PvPvE: other squads board too.

Loadout

  • An anti-ARC gun (Hullcracker, Jupiter) plus a Raider gun
  • Grappling Hook or Snap Hook
  • Healing, shield rechargers, Barricade Kits
  • A Safe Pocket for the module you came for

Loot

Frigate Inductors (needed for Research IV), ARC Data Modules, Amplification Modules, and Frigate Diagnostic Nodes (1,000 RP). None of these are guaranteed.

  1. BoardAt the rear docking area, wait for a circular Reclaimer to come down low, step on it and Disrupt it with the Raider Tool. It carries you up. Its cannon targets the ground, so move when you see the warning lasers.
  2. LiftThe first lift needs players to hit Move Up at the same time. Count down together.
  3. AtriumDon't stand in the middle circle trading shots with several Hydras. Shoot the yellow wall nodes to open climbable sections.
  4. Core 1Use the Move Up panels around the circle to raise the central Power Core, then shoot its weak sections.
  5. Top deckHydras, flying ARC, turbine wind and fire. Clear the nearest threat, then move to the next hard cover.
  6. All coresDestroy every remaining core, including a small one in a lower room under a red-lit hatch. Hatches burn you as they open (since 2.1), so step back.
  7. ReleaseBack at the cargo section, two players hold the release controls on opposite sides at the same time.
  8. Ride downRide the cargo down. Stay clear of the landing spot, where you can still be crushed. Loot it, then walk to a normal exit. The cargo is not an extraction.

Solo run: board, loot the reachable "Breach & Search" containers on the hull, then ride a Reclaimer back down. Celeste's quest "Boarding the Frigate": deal 100 damage each to a Hydra, a Vaporizer and a Rocketeer. Kills aren't required. "Disrupting the Frigate" follows it.

Queen, Matriarch and Harvester

Queen ~23,000 HP

Guards Harvesters when that condition is active. She fires 2–5 mortars that burst into fire after 2 seconds, a laser beam, and an EMP pulse or ground slam up close. She also lasers the Harvester's node while you loot it, which electrocutes everyone inside.

Kill: strip the armor plates, then hit the red core and the yellow leg joints. Damage her between abilities, and throw a Deadline at her head or the exposed core. Since 2.0 the best loot is in the legs, not the armor plates.

Matriarch ~23,000 HP

Same weak points as the Queen, but she summons ARC, both big and small, and adds missile swarms, gas, flashbangs and a shield. Grappling Hooks can't be thrown inside her shield.

Kill: clear the adds first, fight from solid cover, break the faceplates, then focus the core. This is trio content.

Harvester

An ARC structure with electric and fire defenses, guarded by the Queen. It's the source of the Jupiter and Equalizer blueprints; ordinary containers won't drop them.

Fastest squad plan: one player kites the Queen while the others work the Harvester, then everyone collapses on her once her armor is off.
