Shields

Light doesn't slow you down. Medium is the all-rounder. Heavy blocks the most but costs 15% move speed. Your augment decides which shields you can wear.

Patch 2.3 raised the Heavy Shield's base charge from 80 to 90. Its stats below are from before that change.

Augments

Combat, Looting and Tactical lines, Mk 1–3. They set your carry weight, backpack slots, quick-use slots, shield compatibility and Safe Pocket. Looting Mk 3 (Safekeeper) is the one for keeping valuables safe. Higher Mk 3 versions need Gear Bench 3, which takes Bastion Cells.

New in Frozen Trail

  • Grappling Hook: free blueprint. Climb, rappel and swing. It can bounce you into the air (known bug).
  • Snap Hook: the stronger hook, from Utility Station 3. Close to required for the Fallen Emperor.
  • Tether Launcher: wires two points together. Roots targets, or ties a Raider to an ARC.
  • Yank Grenade: pulls a target hard. Drag flyers to the ground or Raiders out of cover.
  • Camera: photo quests and the Outpost unlock.
