Beginner guide

The raid loop

  1. Gear up in Speranza (or take a free loadout).
  2. Pick a map and a goal.
  3. Loot, fight or avoid ARC and other Raiders.
  4. Extract with what you found. If you die, you lose what you brought in, except what's in your Safe Pocket.
  5. Craft, upgrade benches, do quests, repeat.

Free loadouts

A free random kit you can take on any raid without risking your own gear. Use it to learn maps, farm materials and do risky quests. It's disabled during the Frigate event.

Fight or leave?

  • Fight: when you have the jump, cover, and something worth taking.
  • Leave: when you have the item you came for, you're low on heals, or a third squad is close.
  • Gunfire draws ARC and other players, and so does calling an extraction.
  • Ping a Snitch on sight and kill it before it calls reinforcements.

Don't do this

  • Sell quest items (check the Items page first).
  • Recycle materials an Outpost or bench upgrade needs.
  • Bring your best gun on a raid you're only doing for materials.
  • Follow a skill guide from before October 2026.

Events and projects

Soul's Offering Until Nov 16

  1. Stage 1: 1 Dead Flowers, 2 Rock Salt, 15 ARC Alloy, 1 Expired Respirator. Reward: Stiletto II and attachments.
  2. Stage 2: 3 Dead Flowers, 2 Railway Lantern, 15 Advanced ARC Powercell, 10 Remote Raider Flare. Reward: Torrente II and attachments.
  3. Stage 3: 2 Fancy Plate, 1 Mask Fragment, 1 Insulation Roll, 5 Fertilizer. Reward: Vulcano I, Shotgun Silencer, 3 Fireworks Boxes.
  4. Stage 4: set off a Fireworks Box on the Santa Maria Houses roof in Buried City (it has to launch). Reward: Pilgrim Backpack and Offering Display Table.

Dead Flowers and Rock Salt: houses and kitchen drawers in Old Town (Pendola), Grandioso Apartments (Buried City) and Ruined Homestead (Blue Gate).

Expeditions

The season-reset project. Each completed Expedition gives up to 5 permanent skill points (max +15) and other bonuses. Expeditions are paused until early 2027 while Embark reworks them, so nothing you build now gets wiped.

Trials and Reward Pass

Trials are weekly scored objectives; Season 6, Frozen Trials, is live. The Reward Pass (60 levels) replaced Raider Decks. The free track gives 200 Raider Tokens, 5 outfits and 20 cosmetics; Premium adds outfit variants, stencils and 1,150 tokens. The Legacy Pass holds all the old decks.

PvE-only matchmaking

Embark tested a PvE toggle from Oct 13 to 20 (over 30 million PvE rounds). They called the results "polarizing" and said a decision is coming in November 2026.
