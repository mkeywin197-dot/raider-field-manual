Frozen Trail skill tree · 76 points at level 75 · up to +15 from Expeditions

Skill Builds

Three builds, laid out point by point in the order to buy them as you level up. Each one also shows where your Expedition bonus points go. You earn 1 point per level and up to 5 per completed Expedition.

How the tree works

  • Three branches: Conditioning, Mobility, Survival.
  • 10 points in a branch opens its first big node: pick 1 of 2.
  • 30 points opens the capstones: your first pick. 40 points allows a second pick. Max 2 of 3.
  • A node needs at least 1 point in the node before it on its path.

Respec

  • 2,000 Coins per spent point, whole tree only (76 points = 152,000).
  • A respec is the only way to swap a choice node.
  • Ignore guides from before October 2026. Frozen Trail removed 13 skills, including Calming Stroll, Carry The Momentum and Blast-Born.
