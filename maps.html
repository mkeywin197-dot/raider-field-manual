Maps

Where the good loot is on every map, which keys open which rooms, how to leave, and what each map condition changes. High marks the top-tier areas, which are also the most contested.

Plan every raid like this

  1. Pick one goal: a material, a quest item or an ARC part.
  2. First stop near your spawn, then one high-value stop only if it's quiet.
  3. Mark two exits before you go into a hot area.
  4. Leave when the goal is done. A small haul you extract beats a full bag on the ground.

Ways out

Elevator
Dam, Spaceport, Riven Tides. Loud, and you have to wait.
Metro
Buried City, Stella Montis.
Airshaft
Blue Gate, Stella Montis.
Raider Hatch
Needs a Hatch Key. Off during Frigate and Locked Gate, and doesn't exist on Pendola Pass.
Gondola
Pendola Pass only. Call it at a transceiver, then board where it lands.
