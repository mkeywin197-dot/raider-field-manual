Benches

What each Workshop station costs to build and upgrade, and what each level lets you craft. Below that is a shopping list: tick the upgrades you're going for and it adds up every material you need.

Upgrade order: start with the Workbench (free after your first raid). Then build whatever is blocking you: Medical Lab if you're short on healing, Gunsmith for the gun you want, Gear Bench for shields and augments. Each level-3 bench needs parts from a specific ARC, so plan those hunts.
