Amplified Level V

What you need

  • Gunsmith IV: 3 Radial Press, 3 Magnetic Accelerator, 1 Emperor Modulator (Fallen Emperor)
  • Research Station IV: requires Outpost room 4
  • An Amplification Module matching the gun's rarity: Mk I (Common) → Mk V (Legendary). They come from the Fallen Emperor and Frigate cargo.
  • Some amplifications also need an Amplified Research project done first.

Rules

  • Every Level V gun gets +70 max durability, and most also get faster fire, reload or better dispersion recovery.
  • Once activated, an amplification can't be removed or changed. Choose carefully.
  • Some require another one first (e.g. Rattler's Drum Mag needs More Reload); some conflict (e.g. X-Rounds and Full-Auto).
  • Repairs need Amplified Fragments. Downed Raiders drop Amplified guns at 10% durability.

Best picks

  • Anvil: X-Rounds (strips ARC armor)
  • Il Toro: Slug Rounds (a one-shot DMR)
  • Kettle: Full-Auto (a cheap close-range shredder)
  • Rattler: More Reload → Drum Mag (64 rounds)
  • Aphelion: Charged Burst → Increased Burst + Incendiary
  • Renegade: Scoped
