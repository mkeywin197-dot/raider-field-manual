ARC Enemies

The rule that beats almost every ARC: glowing yellow parts are weak points, red on the bosses. Hit thrusters on flying ARC, and leg joints and back canisters on walkers. Armored ARC need heavy ammo or explosives.

Ammo vs ARC armor

Each gun's ammo sets how well it cuts through ARC armor. Bring heavy ammo, launchers or energy weapons for armored ARC (Bastion, Bombardier, Leaper, Queen), and use light or medium ammo on flyers' thrusters.
